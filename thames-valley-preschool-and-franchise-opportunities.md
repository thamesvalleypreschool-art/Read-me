
## Where Does Thames Valley Preschool Fit In?

Thames Valley Preschool is a preschool brand that also offers franchise opportunities.

Whatever age your child starts preschool, apply the same checks to any Thames Valley centre near you that you would use when evaluating any other school. Consider the learning environment, teacher support, safety, hygiene, daily routine, and your child's comfort.

If you are interested in starting your own preschool centre, you can [explore Thames Valley franchise opportunities](https://thamesvalleyglobal.org/).
