
# Right Age to Start Preschool: A Simple Parent Guide

Your neighbour’s child started at two. Your cousin says three is better. The school says “anytime.” What is the right age to start preschool?

There is no single right age for every child. Parents should look for signs of readiness before choosing when to start preschool. This guide explains the important things to consider.

**Disclosure:** This guide is published by [Thames Valley Preschool](https://thamesvalleyglobal.org/). The information is general and can help parents evaluate any preschool.

## How Do You Know the Right Age to Start Preschool?

In India, many children start play school between 2 and 3 years of age. Nursery commonly starts around age 3.

The right time depends on whether your child feels comfortable spending a few hours away from parents in a safe and supportive environment.

Every child develops differently. A confident two-and-a-half-year-old may adjust earlier than a shy three-year-old. Both are normal.

## Key Takeaway

The right age to start preschool depends on your child's development, emotional comfort, communication skills, and ability to adjust to a new environment.

Parents should focus on their child's readiness instead of comparing their age with other children.
