
## How Do Age Cut-Offs and Birth Months Affect Preschool Admission?

Schools usually set an age cut-off date for admission to each class. A child born just before or after that date may be placed in a different class from a friend of a similar age.

Age cut-off dates can differ by school and state. Parents should check the current admission guidelines of each school before applying.

If your child is close to the age cut-off, ask the school how it handles such cases. Some schools may offer flexibility, while others follow fixed admission criteria.

## How Can You Prepare Your Child for the First Day of Preschool?

A little preparation can help children feel more comfortable and confident before starting preschool.

Here are five practical tips for parents:

- **Visit the School:** Take your child to visit the school once or twice before the first day.
- **Maintain a Routine:** Keep sleep and meal times consistent for at least a week before school begins.
- **Practise Short Separations:** Let your child spend short periods with a trusted relative or caregiver.
- **Label Belongings:** Put your child's name on bags, water bottles, and clothes.
- **Keep Goodbyes Calm:** Make your goodbye short, reassuring, and consistent every day.

### Is It Normal for Children to Cry on the First Day?

Yes. Some children may cry, feel anxious, or hesitate to enter the classroom during their first few days.

With patient teachers, a familiar routine, and support from parents, many children gradually become more comfortable. Some settle within a few weeks, while others may need additional time.

Preparing for preschool is not just about choosing the right admission age. Understanding school cut-off dates and helping your child adjust to a new routine can make the transition easier.
