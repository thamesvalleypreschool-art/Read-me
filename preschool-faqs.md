
# Frequently Asked Questions About Preschool Age

### 1. What Is the Right Age to Start Preschool in India?

Many children start play school between 2 and 3 years, while nursery commonly begins around age 3. The right age depends on a child's emotional comfort, development, and ability to adjust to a new environment. NEP 2020 identifies ages 3 to 6 as the preschool stage.

### 2. Is 2 Years Too Early for Preschool?

No, not necessarily. Some children adjust well to play school at age 2, especially when sessions are short and the environment is supportive. Others may need more time before starting.

### 3. Is 3 Years the Best Age for Nursery?

Around 3 years is a common age for nursery admission in India. However, admission age requirements and cut-off dates vary between schools. Parents should check the school's latest admission guidelines.

### 4. Does Starting Preschool Early Give a Child an Advantage?

Starting early does not automatically give a child an advantage. A caring teacher, safe environment, play-based learning, and a comfortable routine are more important than starting at a younger age.

### 5. What If My Child Cries a Lot at Preschool?

Some children cry during their first few days or weeks because they are adjusting to a new environment. Parents can help by keeping goodbyes calm and sharing their child's habits with the teacher. If the distress continues, discuss the situation with the teacher.

### 6. Does My Child Need to Be Toilet Trained Before Preschool?

This depends on the school's admission policy. Some schools expect children to be toilet trained, while others provide support during the process. Always ask about the school's policy before admission.
