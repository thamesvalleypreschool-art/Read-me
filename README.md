
# Right Age to Start Preschool: A Simple Parent Guide

This guide helps parents understand the right age to start preschool in India, recognise signs of school readiness, and prepare their children for their first school experience.

## What This Guide Covers

The article covers the following important topics:

1. **Right Age to Start Preschool** – Understanding when children can begin play school or nursery.

2. **Preschool Age Groups in India** – Typical age ranges for Playgroup, Nursery, LKG, UKG, and Class 1.

3. **NEP 2020 Guidelines** – Understanding the Foundational Stage (ages 3 to 8) and the preschool education structure.

4. **Signs of Preschool Readiness** – Communication, social interaction, following instructions, and emotional comfort.

5. **Is Earlier Always Better?** – Why starting preschool early does not automatically give children an advantage.

6. **What If a Child Is Not Ready?** – Practical options such as waiting, shorter school sessions, and home preparation.

7. **Age Cut-Offs and Birth Month** – How school admission age requirements can affect class placement.

8. **Preparing for the First Day** – Simple ways parents can help children adjust to preschool.

9. **Thames Valley Preschool** – Information about the preschool brand and its franchise opportunities.

10. **Frequently Asked Questions** – Answers to common parent questions about preschool admission age, readiness, separation anxiety, and toilet training.

## Who Is This Guide For?

- Parents planning their child's first preschool admission.
- Families comparing playgroup and nursery admission ages.
- Parents looking for signs of school readiness.
- Families preparing children for their first day at preschool.

## Main Takeaway

The right age to start preschool depends on a child's individual development, emotional readiness, and comfort with a new learning environment. Parents should focus on readiness rather than comparing their child with others.
